Glossary of Terms

Respect
Price touches the zone, exits favorably, and travels at least R points away without first breaking the zone.
Pierce → Revert
Price touches the zone, pierces into it (but not beyond the overshoot O), then reverts to travel R points in the favorable direction.
Break
Price closes beyond the zone plus the overshoot tolerance O.
Timeout
The episode is censored, either by reaching the maximum T bars or by a session boundary change, before a clear outcome is observed.
